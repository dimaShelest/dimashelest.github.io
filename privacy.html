Privacy Policy — autotube

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

autotube is a private tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel The Deep Base for that channel only.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own YouTube account, with two permissions: youtube.upload (to upload the channel's own videos and set their thumbnails) and youtube.readonly (to read the channel id, the list of its uploads and the status of an upload). It accesses no other Google data.

How the data is used and kept

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

Through the channel's About page on YouTube.
